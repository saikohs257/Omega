# TIAMAT Head Semantics — Canonical Memory

**Status:** research/canonical historical memory

**Purpose:** Preserve the recovered meaning and scope of the TIAMAT heads so future work does not collapse them into one generic predictor or forget their topology.

## Core rule

TIAMAT heads are **scoped by transition lineage/topology**. A head is not simply a differently named copy of a global hazard predictor. Its authority comes from the legal transition/mechanism it represents.

## Heads

### H0 — 0→4 entry lineage

Handles the **0→4 entry path**: the low-prior-burden admission route.

It should be interpreted as the mechanism associated with entering Stage 4 from the 0-side lineage, not as a generic "low hazard" predictor.

### H2 — 2→4 entry lineage

Handles the **2→4 entry path**: the intermediate-prior-burden admission route.

Its scope is the 2→4 mechanism, not a universal market/risk score.

### H3 — 3→4 boundary

Handles the **3→4 boundary condition**: the high-prior-burden / preloaded entry state.

H3 is a boundary/lineage head, not simply the "strongest" predictor.

### H4 — 4→4 persistence/release

Handles the **4→4 persistence/release path**: behavior while already in Stage 4, including continued persistence and release/transition behavior.

It is not an entry head.

### ExitBridge — exit/timing machinery

Handles the **exit/timing bridge** toward release. It is separate from the entry-lineage heads and should not be treated as another entry predictor.

### PriorCarry — historical-state carry

Carries **previous-state/history information across transition boundaries**. Its purpose is to preserve the prior burden/context that makes the different entry paths meaningful.

It is memory/continuity machinery, not a universal prediction head.

## Separate scoped components

### T3 — baseline steering

T3 is separate baseline-steering machinery. It is not one of the scoped H0/H2/H3/H4 entry/persistence heads.

### S2 — annotation/diagnostic

S2 is diagnostic/annotation-only. It does not receive canonical authority as an owner head.

## Authority rules

1. Do not merge H0/H2/H3/H4 into a single global score.
2. Do not let one head silently impersonate another transition lineage.
3. Do not use BVD/DVB as canonical state; those are visual/reference surfaces.
4. Do not promote diagnostic/annotation components into authority without an explicit historical decision.
5. Preserve the distinction between entry, persistence, exit/timing, and prior-state carry.
6. If a proposed feature cannot be assigned a legitimate topology/timing seat, treat it as diagnostic until proven otherwise.

## Relationship to the recovered body

The historical body constructs state and lineage first. The heads interrogate specific legal transitions/mechanisms on top of that body:

```text
historical observations
        ↓
primitive state
        ↓
stage / run state
        ↓
entry lineage + persistence history
        ↓
H0 / H2 / H3 / H4
        ↓
ExitBridge / PriorCarry
        ↓
authority firewall
```

The heads therefore must be evaluated against the **exact recovered historical body**, not against synthetic substitutes or visualization-only BVD/DVB representations.

## Provenance note

This document preserves the head semantics recovered during the TIAMAT/Omega archaeological work. It is intentionally explicit about scope because losing the topology distinction was a recurring source of confusion during reconstruction.

If a later artifact contradicts any item here, do not silently overwrite this document. Record the contradiction, identify the newer source, and update the canonical decision with provenance.
