# TIAMAT Research Family Registry V1

Status: research registry; NOT runtime authority; NOT probability authority.

## Purpose

Keep Pathbook/runtime contracts separate from exploratory TIAMAT families. A family can be empirically useful without being promoted to runtime authority.

## Authority states

- OBSERVED: directly present in source data/logs.
- RESEARCH: experimentally constructed/search-derived result.
- SHADOW: replayed candidate, not authorized for runtime.
- CANDIDATE: passed its local research gates but not global promotion.
- PROMOTED: explicitly admitted by a later authority court.
- QUARANTINED: failed a required gate or contains unresolved provenance.
- INCOMPARABLE: cannot participate in the current probability/metric contract.

## Promotion rule

No research family may become Pathbook/runtime authority merely because it wins a local AUC, precision, recall, or court score. Promotion requires:

1. source/provenance verification;
2. frozen feature and label timing;
3. independent chronological replay;
4. pocket/worse-both analysis;
5. comparison with controls/nulls;
6. probability contract compliance if used by the statistical tournament;
7. explicit authority-court promotion.

## Forbidden shortcut

Do not merge multiple research families into a universal scalar. Preserve the question, timing seat, path seat, and family identity that produced each result.
