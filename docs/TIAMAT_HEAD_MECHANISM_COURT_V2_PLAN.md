# TIAMAT Head Mechanism Court V2 — Ten-Step Plan

Research-only. No runtime authority changes.

1. Freeze the 43,848-row historical Layer-1 witness panel and its exact historical outputs.
2. Freeze head-local targets separately from the common 15-hour structural comparator.
3. Partition observations by legal transition seat: H0 0→4, H2 2→4, H3 3→4, H4 4→4, ExitBridge exit/timing, PriorCarry memory.
4. Establish the historical baseline for each seat using only information available before the target.
5. Perform leave-one-variable-out ablations for LiveDeficit, hazard_raw, SimpleShock, timers, and prior-state variables where applicable.
6. Perform pairwise interaction tests and record non-additive effects rather than assuming additive scores.
7. Perform cross-head contamination tests: apply each candidate mechanism outside its legal seat and measure degradation/false activation.
8. Repeat the local tests by year / untouched-year holdout to expose regime dependence and small-sample artifacts.
9. Record negative results and counterexamples; do not promote a head because a blended score improves aggregate AUC.
10. Produce a final head evidence ledger with PROVEN / SUPPORTED / PARTIAL / REJECTED / UNKNOWN status and only then consider any runtime integration.

Current evidence: H3 is strongest in the historical head-local court (AUC 0.9643); H0 0.9429; H2 0.9337; H4 0.7713. The common 15-hour structural target is a separate experiment and must not be conflated with these values. ExitBridge currently outperforms simple PriorCarry for future re-entry timing, but this does not make PriorCarry a generic predictor.
