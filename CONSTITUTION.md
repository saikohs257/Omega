# Omega Constitution

## Axiom Zero
Nothing becomes authoritative merely because it exists. Authority is earned through replay, invariant compliance, and constitutional promotion.

## Prime Directive
Reconstruct hidden state from incomplete observations while preserving replayability and lawful evolution.

## The Three Layers
1. **Executable Substrate:** Deterministic, replayable mechanics. No intelligence.
2. **Authoritative Substrate:** Replay, authority, evidence, identity, truth.
3. **Evolutionary Cognition:** Competing representations, marketplace, selection, learning.

## Omega Maturity Model (OMM)
Every subsystem exists in one of five states:

- **Stage 0 — Concept:** Idea only. No API, tests, or implementation.
- **Stage 1 — Specification:** Jurisdiction, interfaces, invariants, inputs, and outputs defined.
- **Stage 2 — Implementation:** Executable, passes unit tests, interfaces frozen. Not authoritative.
- **Stage 3 — Authoritative:** Sole source of truth for its jurisdiction. Replay-valid. Cannot be bypassed.
- **Stage 4 — Evolutionary:** Oracle may propose lawful changes to its specification or implementation.

## Constitutional Rules
1. BentAxis is the sole constitutional repository of evidence. Every authoritative state must be reconstructable from its append-only evidence log.
2. No direct worker-to-worker communication. All coordination occurs through BentAxis.
3. Immutable events and trajectories.
4. Replay is authoritative.
5. Court decides what is legally admissible; AxisControl selects among admissible representations.
6. No subsystem may reach Stage 3 without passing replay and invariant tests.
7. Layer 3 may not directly mutate Layer 1 or Layer 2 state; it must propose through Oracle.
